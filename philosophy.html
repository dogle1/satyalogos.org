The Satyalogos Program

Philosophy

The Satyalogos philosophy is developed in two registers. The quantitative philosophy is the formal, structural argument — relation before relata, built with the discipline of a rigorous paper and held to what can be defended in the third person. The qualitative philosophy is the first-person, phenomenological account of the same reality from the inside. They are one vision at two epistemic postures: the paper below is the official articulation, and the lived account is its companion.

Open Access · 2026

Relation Before Relata: A Relational-Primitivist Ontology of Individuation, Perspective, and Presence

Dustin Ogle · Satyalogos Inc. · 2026 — the quantitative philosophy

Develops relational primitivism: relational organization is prior to the entities within it, and a thing is a pattern in a relational structure stable enough to be individuated as one. From a single relational primitive and four openly stated posits it builds individuation, persistence, and the localized perspectives from which a world appears, then decomposes the overloaded terms of the debate — consciousness, experience, observer — to isolate the one genuinely hard residue, intrinsic presence, which it types as an open trilemma and argues structural description cannot settle. A closing section reads rival total pictures as partial resolutions of one structure. The argument is meant to stand even if every proper name were struck out, and its exact, machine-verified formal companion is the Sp(3) relational construction.

Satyalogos — The Qualitative Philosophy

Dustin Ogle · Satyalogos · the first-person account

The same vision described from the inside: a first-person, phenomenological account of fundamental reality — the Axiom, the Union, the Mechanism, the Veil, and what the awakening discloses. Where the quantitative philosophy holds presence open on principle, the qualitative one speaks from lived conviction. Read it as the committed, experiential companion to the formal paper above.
